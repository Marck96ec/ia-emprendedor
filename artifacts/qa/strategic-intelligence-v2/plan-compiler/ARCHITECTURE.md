# Plan Compiler Architecture — TuCEO IA v2.7

**Date:** 2026-11-26
**Branch:** feature/strategic-intelligence-v2

---

## Problem Statement

The current CEO plan generator (`generate-ceo-plan.ts`) asks the AI to produce a complete plan object that includes both reasoning-intensive fields and fields that are deterministically derivable from business inputs. This creates several problems:

1. The AI is asked to compute values (e.g., `time_allocation.available_hours`) that are always overwritten post-generation — wasted tokens and a potential source of hallucination.
2. When a plan is rejected, it's impossible to tell whether the rejection is due to AI reasoning or a deterministic field that could be repaired in TypeScript.
3. The contract between AI and TypeScript is implicit — fields are silently corrected without signaling the correction.

---

## Concept: Two-Layer Architecture

The Plan Compiler is a conceptual separation of the CEO plan pipeline into two layers:

### Reasoning Layer (AI)
The AI concentrates exclusively on fields that require business judgment:

- `strategic_diagnosis.*` — synthesis of business context into diagnosis, bottleneck, evidence, hypothesis, confidence, unknowns, decision
- `strategic_continuity` — narrative thread connecting last week's decisions to this week's plan
- `weekly_plan[].action`, `.objective`, `.reason`, `.success_metric`, `.expected_result` — natural language plan content
- `weekly_plan[].strategy.justification`, `.prior_barrier` — strategic explanation
- `weekly_plan[].capture_schema.fields[]` — form fields for capturing execution evidence
- `priorities[].title`, `.reason`, `.expected_impact` — business prioritization narrative
- `strategic_diagnosis.evidence[].interpretation` — connecting observed facts to strategic meaning

### Compilation Layer (TypeScript)
TypeScript handles fields where the information is unambiguously derivable from inputs:

| Field | Derivation rule |
|-------|----------------|
| `time_allocation.available_hours` | `diagnostic.ownerHoursPerWeek` — always |
| `time_allocation.allocated_hours` | `sum(weekly_plan[].estimated_hours)` — always |
| `time_allocation.reserved_hours` | `available - allocated` — always |
| `strategy.work_key` | `inferWorkKey(action.title)` — when unambiguous |
| `strategy.deliverable` | `commercialDeliverableFor(action.title, strategy)` — normalization |
| `strategy.intent` | `inferredIntent(action.title)` — for ai_assisted/build types |
| `strategic_diagnosis.confidence` | Downgrade when `unknowns.length > 1` and `confidence === "high"` |
| `strategy.depends_on_sequence` | Promote `completed_prerequisite_id` when pointing to current-plan pending action |

## Phase 1 (This Release — v2.7)

**Fields extracted from AI responsibility:**

None removed from the AI schema yet. `time_allocation.*` fields are still generated by the AI but always overwritten post-generation (lines 587–603 of `generate-ceo-plan.ts`). The extraction is conceptual: TypeScript is the authoritative source for these values.

**What changed in v2.7:**
- Shared budget enforcement covering all provider entry points (`provider-budget.ts`)
- Budget consumed before each API call, preventing overspend across all generators
- Observability extended: `PROVIDER_BUDGET_LOG` entries for all provider calls
- E2E checkpoint resume support via `QA_START_WEEK`

## Phase 2 (Future)

**Candidates for removal from AI schema:**

1. `time_allocation.*` — 100% deterministic, safe to make AI-optional or remove entirely
2. `execution_mode` — derivable from `action_type`: `ai_assisted` ↔ `value_proposition`/`commercial_messages`, `manual` for all others
3. `strategy.deliverable` — normalization is already done in TypeScript; AI generating it adds noise

**Not safe to remove yet:**
- `strategy.work_key` — AI may be more accurate than `inferWorkKey()` for ambiguous titles
- `strategy.intent` — AI context may distinguish intent better than heuristics
- `strategic_diagnosis.confidence` — AI assessment is the starting point; TypeScript only adjusts downward

---

## Decision: When to Promote a Field to TypeScript

A field should be fully owned by TypeScript when ALL of the following are true:

1. The field can be computed from business inputs without domain reasoning
2. The AI-generated value is always discarded or adjusted to match the TypeScript computation
3. The AI generating the field adds no information and wastes tokens
4. Historical plans can be migrated without behavioral change

By these criteria, `time_allocation.*` is the strongest Phase 2 candidate.
